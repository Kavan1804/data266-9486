Status: All code cells ran with no errors and no warnings. Training took 5839.2 s (97.3 min).
Final epoch loss 0.05244, test MSE 1.28678 -> 0.05245. Three problems were found when
reviewing the outputs:
  1. The comparison grid's suptitle still overlapped the panel titles. The suptitle was removed
     and "same x_T" was moved into each panel title.
  2. The comparison table had an "outside [-1, 1]" column, and the markdown described a high
     value as a sign of overshoot. DDPM had 25.07% of pixels outside [-1, 1], but its output
     range was only [-1.04, 1.04]: these were background pixels slightly below -1, which become
     black after clamping. The column was replaced by gray_fraction (pixels between 0.1 and 0.9
     on the [0, 1] scale), which actually measures speckle/blur.
  3. The DDIM samples were worse than DDPM. To check whether this came from the model or from
     the sampling, a diagnostic cell was added: both samplers are started from a real x_100
     (made by the forward process) instead of pure noise, and DDIM 20 is also run with eta = 1.
     A sentence in Section 9 that said the per-batch loss was noisy because small-t batches are
     harder was softened, because the notebook does not measure that.
